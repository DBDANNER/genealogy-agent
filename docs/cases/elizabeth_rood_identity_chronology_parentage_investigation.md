## Executive conclusion

FamilySearch L2F1-G68 is strongly supported as **Elizabeth [maiden surname unknown]**, wife of **Stephen Rude/Rood Jr.**, mother of a coherent Connecticut child sequence extending from at least 1772 through 1793, and probably the **Elizabeth Rood who died 8 February 1820 at age 70** in the Killingly cemetery context.

The apparent chronology crisis that triggered this broken-branch case was not historical. The inherited project summary had treated **1771 as Elizabeth's death**, but FamilySearch profile history shows no 1771 death event. The only 1771 datum is an unsourced estimated marriage. Seven distinct Connecticut birth events from 1772 through 1793 consistently name Stephen Rude/Rood and mother Elizabeth. The supposed posthumous-birth contradiction therefore disappears.

A photographed Hale cemetery abstract records Elizabeth Rood dying **8 February 1820**, age 70, in a Killingly cemetery context; the Old South Killingly transcription identifies her as **wife of Stephen**. Ordinary-Chat peer review grades this death **strongly supported**, not fully established, because the wife-of-Stephen linkage depends in part on the cemetery-transcription chain.

No marriage, child, cemetery, probate, church, land, or original town record reviewed in either phase calls this Elizabeth Harris. A bounded Phase 2 tested the finite remaining marriage, deed, probate, original-town-record, church, and child-backward routes. No maiden-surname or natal-family bridge emerged.
