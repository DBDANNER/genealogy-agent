# Phebe Hamilton Wife of Jonathan — Identity and Parentage Investigation

**FamilySearch PID:** 9VWG-63Y  
**Status:** PEER-REVIEWED — ADULT WIFE/MOTHER IDENTITY STRONGLY SUPPORTED; MAIDEN SURNAME AND PARENTAGE ONLINE-EXHAUSTED UNRESOLVED  
**Peer review completed:** 26 September 2026

## Executive conclusion

The historical woman represented by FamilySearch 9VWG-63Y is strongly supported at very high confidence in a deliberately narrow adult identity:

**Phebe, wife of Jonathan Hamilton in Horton Township, Kings County, Nova Scotia, and mother of Sarah Hamilton born 5 May 1762.**

The strongest direct evidence located is a legacy FamilySearch index entry in *Canada, Nova Scotia, Births and Baptisms, 1702–1896*, naming **Jonathan Hamilton** and **Phebe** as parents of Sarah Hamilton, born 5 May 1762 at Horton. This is strong identity evidence, but the exact original register image has not yet been tied to the index entry. For that reason, ordinary-Chat peer review grades the adult wife/mother identity **strongly supported / very high confidence**, rather than fully established.

A 1918 genealogy citing the Horton town book independently supports a Jonathan-and-Phebe household for a Sarah Hamilton born 24 March 1769. Secondary county-history material also places the older Jonathan Hamilton in the Horton settlement.

No reviewed record establishes Phebe's maiden surname, parents, exact birth, or exact death.

The inherited claims that she was born **about 1734 in New London, Connecticut**, and died **26 July 1786**, are unsupported. The surname **Rathbone** and proposed parents **Joshua Rathbone + Jemima Baldwin** derive from a recent unsourced GEDCOM profile and should not be adopted.

The reasonable finite online road has been exhausted for the current project scope.

**Final natal conclusion: maiden surname unknown; father unknown; mother unknown.**

## 3. Husband

Jonathan Hamilton is strongly supported as Phebe's husband/partner in the Horton household.

The Sarah 1762 index names Jonathan and Phebe together as parents.

Arthur Wentworth Hamilton Eaton's *History of Kings County, Nova Scotia* (1910) identifies an older Jonathan Hamilton as a Horton grantee, reports his death on 24 February 1778, and identifies James Hamilton, born 2 February 1764, as his son.

A later Jonathan Hamilton appears in the 1791 Horton poll-tax material, so not every Horton Jonathan record should be assigned automatically to the same man.

### Final grade

**Jonathan Hamilton as husband of the target Phebe: Strongly supported.**
