# Maria Elisabeth Loux/Laux Huthmacher — Identity and Parentage Investigation

## Executive conclusion

The adult identity represented by FamilySearch KNV1-BCH is securely stabilized as **Maria Elisabeth Loux/Laux**, wife of **Adam Huthmacher/Hutmacher**, married 28 October 1760, and mother of four children documented in Stone Arabia church material between 1762 and 1769.

Her premarital surname **Loux/Laux** is strongly supported, although spelling varies across records and derivatives.

The inherited assertions that she was born **about 1741** and specifically at **Stone Arabia, New York**, are unsupported. Neither should be used as a firm natal fact in later migration, chronology, or origin-composition analysis.

No reviewed source identifies her parents.

The previously attached **Johann Adam Laux + Catherine Elisabeth Schnell** parentage is contradicted by the separately documented adult life of their daughter Elisabeth Barbara, who married Jacob Seever in 1766.

The strongest remaining natal-family hypothesis is the household of **Johann Henrich Laux + Anna Margaretha**, because a derivative reconstruction places Peter, Maria, and Maria Barbara in that family, while Huthmacher child baptisms use sponsors including a Pieter explicitly described as son of Henrich Laux Sr. and a Barbara Laux. This is meaningful FAN evidence but does not establish filiation. The candidate household is **plausible only** and may itself have been reconstructed partly from the same sponsor network, creating a circularity risk.

After a targeted Phase 2 review of sponsor-linked households, accessible probate/estate, land/property, church, and Revolutionary-era relational material, the reasonable online road is exhausted for this project's scope.

**Final parentage conclusion: father unknown; mother unknown.**

- Maria Barbara Laux, about 1730–1794
- Maria Laux, about 1743–deceased
- Peter Laux, about 1743–deceased

Why this matters:

- the 1762 Huthmacher baptism explicitly calls sponsor Pieter the unmarried son of **Henrich Laux Sr.**
- the 1765 baptism includes **Barbara Laux**
- the derivative household contains **Peter, Maria, and Maria Barbara**
- a proposed daughter Maria is chronologically compatible with a 1760 marriage

Why this does not prove parentage:

- the household reconstruction is derivative
- the proposed Maria and Peter lack independent relational records in the reviewed material
- no record names Maria Elisabeth Huthmacher as daughter or sister
- Jones may itself have used sponsor or similar association evidence in reconstructing the household, creating possible circularity

### Final grade

**Johann Henrich Laux + Anna Margaretha as parents of Maria Elisabeth Huthmacher: plausible only / leading candidate household.**

They must not be attached as parents on the strength of the present evidence.
